## The problem

In a grid-based kitchen game, chefs must collect and prepare ingredients and deliver finished dishes against a stream of orders. The task was to write a controller that chooses every chef's actions.

## What I built

A controller that expresses the kitchen as a PDDL planning problem and lets an automated planner decide what the chefs do, evaluated on the course's levels. This is a coursework assessment, so I describe the approach here and do not publish the code.
